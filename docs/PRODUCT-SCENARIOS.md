# Product scenarios

Bot Hub supports product-focused sales bots without forcing operators to design an n8n graph.

## Product quick setup

In **Create Bot → Teach**, choose a Product scenario and enter:

- product name;
- verified current price, when available;
- short introduction / customer problem solved;
- highlights and practical benefits;
- CTA / next step;
- additional warranty, delivery, promotion and policy knowledge when available.

The UI stores this as bot-specific Product Knowledge. Product rules and AI prompts are not allowed to invent missing facts.

## Product Introduction

Recommended flow:

```text
Customer asks about a product
  ↓
product-intro intent
  ↓
Product Introduction scenario
  ↓
Product Knowledge
  ↓
AI-assisted grounded response
  ├─ What it is
  ├─ Customer value / problem solved
  ├─ Highlights
  ├─ Practical benefits
  ├─ Who it fits
  ├─ Verified price (only if present)
  └─ CTA
```

Pricing and promotion questions are also knowledge-grounded. A missing price or promotion is reported as missing data rather than generated by the model.

## Product Advisor

Recommended flow:

```text
Need / budget / priority
  ↓
Product Advisor
  ├─ product-recommendation
  ├─ product-compare
  ├─ product-intro
  └─ pricing
  ↓
Compare only verified product fields
  ↓
Recommendation + reason
  ↓
Lead capture / human handoff
```

A comparison needs enough knowledge for the requested products. Missing attributes must be marked as unavailable rather than inferred.

## Hybrid behavior

`Hybrid` is recommended for product bots:

- deterministic rules control handoff, lead capture and business boundaries;
- AI-assisted scenario rules create natural product explanations using the selected bot's knowledge;
- outside the scenario, the general AI router can answer from knowledge;
- when no AI provider is configured, the local fallback can still introduce a product from Product Knowledge.

## Data quality rules

Never invent or silently infer:

- price;
- promotion / voucher / expiry;
- inventory / stock;
- technical specifications;
- warranty;
- shipping promise;
- order status.

If these facts are not present in current business data, say that they are not confirmed yet or hand off to a human.
